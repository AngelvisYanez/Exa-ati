# OFSERCONT IA — Asistente Tributario Inteligente

## Product

Product dashboard and tax management suite for Ecuadorian accountants and businesses. Users manage SRI (Servicio de Rentas Internas) electronic documents, tax declarations, accounting, and AI-powered tax assistance — all in one platform.

## Audience

Ecuadorian accountants, tax advisors, and business owners who need to:
- Sync and manage SRI electronic invoices/receipts
- Emit authorized tax documents (facturas, retenciones, notas de crédito/débito, guías de remisión)
- Calculate and present IVA, ICE, and income tax declarations
- Manage chart of accounts and tax positions
- Get AI-powered tax consultation via chat

## Brand

- **Name:** OFSERCONT IA (branded as EXA-ATI)
- **Lane:** Product (Operate mode — design serves the task)
- **Tone:** Professional, trustworthy, efficient. Users handle money and legal compliance — the interface must inspire confidence through clarity, not decoration.
- **Visual system:** Clean, restrained, data-dense. Cards, tables, charts, and forms are the primary surfaces. Color is used sparingly for state (success/warning/error) and brand accent (burgundy red #a02525).
- **Anti-references:** Over-decorated fintech dashboards, dark mode as default, purple/blue gradient accents, glassmorphism, card-nested-in-card layouts.

## Design constraints

- Operate mode: users are in a task. Familiarity and consistency matter more than surprise.
- Light theme only (Ecuadorian offices are brightly lit; dark mode is not the primary use scene).
- Data density is a feature: tables with many rows, panels with many labels.
- Motion at 150–250ms, state-driven only (no orchestrated page-load sequences).
- Typography: one sans family (DM Sans), fixed rem scale, tight ratio (1.125). Mono for RUC/claves: DM Mono.
